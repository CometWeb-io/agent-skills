# Competitive Landscape Movement — Q3 2026

**As of:** 2026-10-01T12:00:00+02:00
**Competitors covered:** acme, betacorp, gammasoft, deltaworks (snapshots from 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30)

## Market direction

Packaging is moving down-market: security and admin features that were Enterprise-only are reaching mid tiers.

## Convergence

Mid-tier plans now look alike: SSO, audit log and a seat floor of 5 appear in three of four mid tiers.

## Divergence

Pricing units are splitting. Two competitors price per seat; the other two moved to usage-based pricing on tracked pages.

## White-space implications

Hypothesis: no competitor offers a small-team plan with an audit log below 30 USD/month. That gap needs a demand check before it is treated as an opportunity.

## Threats to our assumptions

Our assumption that SSO justifies an Enterprise upgrade is weaker than a quarter ago. It deserves a re-check with win/loss notes, not a price change.

## Evidence strength

- Down-market packaging: 3 competitors, 4 time points each, source classes A (pricing and plan pages) and B (changelogs).
- Mid-tier convergence: 3 competitors, 2 time points each, source class A (plan comparison pages).
- Pricing-unit divergence: 4 competitors, 3 time points each, source classes A and B; the two usage-based moves were checked against invoices shown in public docs.
