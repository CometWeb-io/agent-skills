# Claim Check — "betacorp raised its Team plan price to 39 USD/month"

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED
**As of:** 2026-10-02T11:00:00+02:00

## Direct evidence

- EV-01 — betacorp pricing page (first-party, class A), example.com/betacorp/pricing, captured 2026-10-02T08:20Z: Team plan at 39 USD/month, monthly billing.
- EV-02 — betacorp changelog entry dated 2026-09-29 (first-party, class A), example.com/betacorp/changelog: "new Team pricing for new customers".

## Contradictory or limiting evidence

- EV-03 — a reseller listing (class C), example.net/betacorp-team, still shows 29 USD/month; it was last updated 2026-08-14, before the change, and does not contradict the first-party pages.
- Existing customers keep 29 USD/month until renewal (EV-02); the claim is true for new customers only.

## Scope qualifiers

New customers, US pricing page, monthly billing. EU pricing was not checked in this memo.

## Conclusion

CONFIRMED for new US customers on monthly billing, from two first-party sources captured on the same day.

## What would change the verdict

- A first-party EU or annual price at 29 USD would narrow the claim's scope, not reverse it.
- A retraction notice on the changelog would move the verdict to RETRACTED.
