# Profile result contract v2

Return the complete canonical owner artifact plus this local sidecar; never delegate essential artifact content to it. Full response follows response.schema.json: selected_skills is exactly the single owner, selected_profile_id is the profile ID, artifact is the complete owner output, sidecar follows output.schema.json. Both schemas are closed. Profile IDs cannot be dispatched as skills.

DRAFT/BLOCKED preserve missing inputs; REVIEWABLE requires local completeness, never factual truth or execution permission. execution_authorized is false. Every reference must resolve. Framework guidance goes in result.framework_source_ids, whose references have kind FRAMEWORK. Empirical observations and confirmed process steps require USER_INPUT/OBSERVED. Hypothesis/planned-step references may describe a proposal, never validate a product outcome. Unknowns prevent REVIEWABLE.

Experimental numeric fields have individual parameter_source_ids. A supplied alpha/power is a design input, not a measured product result: link it to the supplying USER_INPUT. Method guidance may cite FRAMEWORK separately. Unknown numeric inputs stay null. The helper does not verify sample-size calculations; their validation remains a declared task dependency. Never coerce booleans, objects or strings into numeric values or verification text.

SOP steps declare basis CONFIRMED or PROPOSED, and an external step needs a permission checkpoint in either case. This contract cannot authorize sending or executing a procedure. The local ledger separately validates the full owner response, wrapper, profile/source lock and completion proof. Completion permits a checkpoint, not external action or a business/release verdict.
