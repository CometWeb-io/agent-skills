# Statistics and uncertainty

Do not infer reliability from one stochastic run. STANDARD promotion claims require at least 3 runs per case; DEEP requires at least 5. Report raw numerators/denominators alongside percentages.

Prefer simple, auditable metrics:
- task pass rate;
- trigger precision and recall;
- invariant regression count;
- token/cost ratio;
- duration ratio;
- pass@k when run-level outcomes are actually recorded.

Do not hide ungradeable assertions inside the denominator.
