# Partner lifecycle and learning loop

## 1. Discovery

Confirm pain, status quo, why now, user/champion, implementation constraints, and the partner's motivation. Distinguish polite interest from a real operational problem.

## 2. Mutual qualification

Use Stage B scoring. Resolve the highest-value unknowns before promising access, custom work, or a pilot.

## 3. Charter and kickoff

Agree roles, learning hypotheses, implementation prerequisites, cadence, success/stop criteria, and bespoke boundaries. Capture any legal/security/privacy triggers.

## 4. Instrument the pilot

Prefer observable behavior:

- activation/implementation progress,
- repeated use of the target workflow,
- task completion/failure,
- time-to-value,
- manual/founder assistance required,
- support burden,
- qualitative feedback tied to specific events,
- buyer vs user divergence.

## 5. Triage requests

Classify each material request:

- `CORE` — broadly transferable and aligned with the product thesis.
- `SEGMENT` — valuable within a deliberately targeted segment.
- `EDGE` — valid but not roadmap-defining.
- `BESPOKE` — primarily serves one partner.
- `CONTRADICTS_THESIS` — signals that the current product/ICP assumption may be wrong.

For material build decisions, record:

- partner(s) requesting it,
- observed workflow evidence,
- transferability evidence,
- expected product value,
- effort/opportunity cost,
- decision and reason.

One prestigious request is not consensus.

## 6. Periodic review

Track at least:

- engagement/usage,
- learning yield,
- implementation progress,
- transferability of feedback,
- support/manual burden,
- bespoke pressure,
- blockers,
- commercial signal separately from learning quality.

Run `scripts/assess_partner_health.py` for a consistent review when useful.

## 7. Graduation

Use:

- `CONTINUE` — strong learning and engagement.
- `REPAIR` — valuable but fixable execution/engagement issue.
- `PAUSE` — timing/capacity blocks useful progress.
- `EXIT_REVIEW` — weak learning, persistent non-use, high bespoke pressure, or disproven fit.
- `CONVERSION_CANDIDATE` — product value is demonstrated and normal commercial qualification should begin.

Conversion is a separate sales decision. Hand it to the appropriate sales/prospecting/revops workflow rather than keeping the account indefinitely in "design partner" status.

## Program-level metrics

Do not optimize only for conversion rate. Track:

- confirmed-pain rate after live discovery,
- activation rate,
- active-usage rate,
- hypothesis coverage and replications,
- learning yield per partner,
- median implementation/support burden,
- partner drop/pause reasons,
- bespoke-request ratio,
- number of product decisions supported or falsified,
- conversion candidates after demonstrated value.
