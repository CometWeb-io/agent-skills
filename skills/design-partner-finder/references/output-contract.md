Adapt depth to the mode. Keep the decision visible before raw research detail.

## 1. Learning Contract

State:

- product stage,
- engagement motion,
- learning strategy,
- target workflow/segment,
- hypotheses and importance,
- must-cover questions,
- capacity constraint,
- disqualifiers.

## 2. Evidence readiness

Summarize:

- material evidence found,
- critical inferred/unknown claims,
- stale/unknown current claims,
- contradiction-search coverage,
- any binding evidence gap.

## 3. Research-stage shortlist

| Rank | Company | Stage A status | Fit score | Why now | Hypotheses covered | Evidence confidence | Key uncertainty | Recommended action |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|---|---:|---|---|

Do not label a research-stage company `PARTNER_READY`.

## 5. Live Partner Readiness

Only include after direct evidence exists.

| Company | Stage B status | Readiness score | Problem confirmed | User+champion | Implementation | Feedback commitment | Authority/procurement | Transferability | Blocker |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Show source/meeting/date for live-confirmed facts when available.

## 6. Cohort portfolio

For each selected company state:

- selection role,
- unique hypotheses covered,
- replication contribution,
- segment/variation role,
- expected implementation/support cost,
- overlap with other partners,
- why it belongs in the cohort rather than merely on the outreach slate.

Then show:

- must-cover hypotheses satisfied / uncovered,
- desired replication achieved / missing,
- segment concentration,
- capacity warning,
- candidates excluded by cohort constraints.

## 7. Rejections and near misses

Keep concise but preserve:

- candidate,
- status,
- rejection/hold reason,
- evidence that caused it,
- what change would justify reconsideration.

## 8. Activation / review output

For ACTIVATE or REVIEW mode add:

- partner charter summary,
- pilot success/failure criteria,
- current usage/implementation state,
- request-triage summary,
- learning yield,
- bespoke/support burden,
- next review date,
- lifecycle state: `CONTINUE`, `REPAIR`, `PAUSE`, `EXIT_REVIEW`, or `CONVERSION_CANDIDATE`.

## 9. Reproducibility

Always include when external discovery materially shaped the answer:

- as-of date/time,
- search/connector scope,
- major source limitations,
- evidence not verified,
- whether research was candidate-complete or stopped by diminishing returns.

## Honest language

Use:

- `candidate design partner`
- `priority discovery candidate`
- `public evidence suggests`
- `live-confirmed`
- `potential fit`
- `needs validation`
- `unverified`

Avoid unsupported claims such as:

- `interested`
- `ready to buy`
- `committed`
- `will convert`
- `agreed to partner`
- `fraudulent/fake` without authoritative evidence.
