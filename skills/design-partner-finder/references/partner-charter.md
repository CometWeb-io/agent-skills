## Mutual commitments

### Partner commitments

- implementation/data/system access within agreed boundaries,
- named users,
- usage expectations,
- feedback cadence,
- candid failure/objection reporting,
- decision/procurement participation when relevant.

### Company commitments

- product/founder access,
- implementation/support scope,
- response cadence,
- roadmap boundaries,
- experiment/iteration expectations,
- commercial terms only when appropriate.

## Success and stop criteria

Define:

- activation threshold,
- value/success signal,
- failure/falsifier,
- maximum acceptable implementation burden,
- bespoke-request threshold,
- pause/exit trigger,
- next review date.

## Issues requiring qualified review when material

Do not hardcode jurisdiction-specific conclusions. Surface and route as needed:

- confidentiality / NDA,
- ownership or license of feedback and resulting IP,
- pre-existing IP,
- use of partner/customer data,
- DPA/privacy obligations,
- security requirements and incident handling,
- access to production systems,
- warranties/SLA/liability/indemnity,
- publicity, logo, case-study, or reference rights,
- exclusivity / competitive restrictions,
- pricing/discount/renewal/transition terms,
- termination and data return/deletion.

A commercial contract can be useful evidence of commitment in some motions, but it is not a universal prerequisite for learning.
