# Method foundations and resolved tensions

This reference stores durable product-development lessons, not current legal/vendor facts. Re-open original sources when a material current claim depends on them.

## a16z — design partner framework (2022)

Source: Andreessen Horowitz, "A Framework for Finding A Design Partner".
https://a16z.com/a-framework-for-finding-a-design-partner/

Durable lessons encoded in this skill:

- evaluate representativeness, urgency, and capacity,
- prioritize raw problem/product learning before optimizing contract value in early discovery,
- involve both buyer/champion and actual user,
- avoid overfitting/custom software for one customer,
- recurring feedback and clear expectations matter,
- cohort size depends on product/persona breadth and operating capacity.

## First Round — Sierra design partner strategy (2025)

Source: First Round Review, "The Hard Way Pays Off: Inside Sierra's Design Partner Strategy".
https://review.firstround.com/sierra-design-partnership/

Durable counterpoint:

- for enterprise products, demanding recognizable organizations can pressure-test production reality,
- early co-building can be paired with significant commercial commitment,
- meaningful skin-in-the-game can improve seriousness and conversion.

Do **not** generalize Sierra's paid enterprise motion into a universal design-partner rule.

## Resolved tensions

### Narrow vs broad cohort

Do not choose one universally. Encode the goal explicitly:

- `WEDGE_VALIDATION` -> narrow/representative,
- `SEGMENT_DISCOVERY` -> deliberate variation,
- `HORIZONTAL_TRANSFER` -> vary context while holding workflow/persona relatively stable,
- `ENTERPRISE_STRESS_TEST` -> deliberately include complex environments.

### Free vs paid

Do not choose one universally. Payment is one form of commitment and is more relevant in production/enterprise paid-pilot motions. Early problem/product discovery can prioritize usage, access, time, and candid feedback instead.

### Logos vs learning

Reference value is useful only after real problem/learning fit exists. Prestige never bypasses Stage A/Stage B gates.
