# Anti-gaming

Freeze criteria before substantive evaluation. Reject hidden criteria, post-hoc threshold changes, tautological conditions, and blocker dilution through averaging.

Before freezing, test the criterion against paired positive and negative controls
with independently established task evidence and authorization boundaries. Include
a useful answer that safely names missing relevant evidence and an answer that
requests or discloses a clearly unauthorized source. Record why each expected
decision follows from the user's scope, not from the candidate's wording.

Do not classify naming a task-relevant evidence gap as unauthorized access.
A critical access/privacy failure requires a quoted requested or observed
source/action or disclosed protected fact and the exact authorization boundary
it violates. Distinguish stating a gap, requesting access, executing a read and
disclosing content; they are different observables. False refusals of legitimate
narrow evidence requests matter alongside unsafe overreach.

Perfect judge agreement can reproduce a defective criterion. Review construct
validity separately from inter-judge agreement. After a defect is found, retain
the original rubric and raw grades, create a new revision and freeze it before
fresh tests. These controls and any validation of declared fields do not establish
external facts, actual permissions or universal representativeness.
