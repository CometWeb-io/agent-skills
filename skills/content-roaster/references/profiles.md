# Content review profiles

Use one profile to bias the failure search. Profiles do not replace the shared evidence protocol.

## `GENERAL`

Use when no more specific artifact type fits. Reconstruct problem -> promise -> mechanism -> proof -> objection -> action.

## `LANDING_PAGE`

Prioritize first-screen comprehension, category/ICP clarity, message hierarchy, mechanism, proof before commitment, objection sequence, and CTA proportionality.

## `PRODUCT_PAGE`

Prioritize job-to-be-done clarity, feature-to-outcome translation, technical specificity, limitations, integration/fit evidence, and buyer proof.

## `PRICING`

Prioritize package boundaries, entitlement clarity, price/value logic, hidden assumptions, upgrade/downgrade implications, risk reversal, comparison fairness, and proof near high-cost choices.

## `OFFER`

Prioritize problem urgency, outcome specificity, mechanism, scope, constraints, deliverables, risk, proof, price/commitment, objections, and next step.

## `EMAIL`

Prioritize relevance, opening context, specificity, credibility, cognitive load, one clear ask, and mismatch between relationship stage and requested commitment.

## `ARTICLE`

Prioritize thesis, evidence, logical progression, novelty/usefulness, scope control, examples, redundancy, and whether title/intro overpromise the body.

## `CASE_STUDY`

Prioritize baseline/context, intervention/mechanism, measurable result, attribution discipline, customer specificity, transferability limits, and unsupported causal framing.

## `SALES_DECK`

Prioritize narrative sequence, audience/problem fit, proof timing, competitive differentiation, economic argument, objection handling, and slide-to-slide decision momentum.

## `LONGFORM`

Prioritize information architecture, evidence placement, concept repetition, chapter promises, practical density, transitions, and whether conclusions exceed the body.

## `DOCUMENTATION`

Prioritize prerequisites, task success path, exact commands/examples, error states, version assumptions, edge cases, and recovery/failure guidance.

## Decision cost

- `LOW`: read, click, subscribe, low-risk exploratory action.
- `MEDIUM`: book a call, create an account, share work contact data, invest meaningful time.
- `HIGH`: purchase, sign contract, migrate, grant access, commit team/process, or rely on consequential claims.
- `UNKNOWN`: insufficient context.

Higher decision cost increases proof and objection-handling burden. It does not automatically make copy weaker.
