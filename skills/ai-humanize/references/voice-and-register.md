# Voice and register

Resolve style conflicts in this order: explicit user instructions, destination, supplied style sample, intentional source voice, generic preferences. Source and sample remain separate: the sample does not supply facts, anecdotes or claims.

Retain clear distinctive wording, technical names, deliberate repetition and formal phrasing that carries meaning. Fix the trait the user asked to change; do not impose sentence-length quotas, fragments, slang, invented errors or punctuation bans. A stronger rewrite need not sound more casual.

If a distinctive phrase from a sample is not ordinary language, describe the voice rather than copying it. Preserve ambiguity or ask about a consequential conflict instead of silently inventing the author's position.
