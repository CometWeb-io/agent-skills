# Corey capability packs — local pilot

Opt-in registry: `registry/capability-packs.json` at the package root.
The orchestrator's `--with-capability-packs` attaches integrity-checked pointers.
Map customer-research to `product_customer`, pricing to `offer_pricing`, offers
to `sales`. Do not select by role alone: the question must also need the pack.
Read only the selected SKILL.md and relevant references. Referenced uninstalled
skills are optional suggestions; never install or invoke them automatically.

These are versioned FRAMEWORK materials. They can supply questions, alternatives,
failure modes and experiments; they are not independent evidence of demand,
conversion lift, willingness to pay or a valid final Council verdict.

Imported guidance is subordinate to supplied facts and Council evidence policy.
In particular, offers' claimed 10–40% lift, 2–3x cumulative lift and churn ratios
are not measured effects for this case. Do not transfer them into a forecast.
Do not invent guarantees, customers, prices, scarcity, baseline metrics or proof.
No outreach, paid acquisition, account mutation or publishing is authorized.

Retain blind rounds, dissent, evidence admission, risk gates and Decision Memory
timing. A pack is a capability of a role, not an extra independent expert.

Every specialist handoff identifies its pack/source commit and separates:
`observed_facts` (input/source locators), `assumptions`, `framework_suggestions`,
`unknowns`, `proposed_tests` (metric, baseline, decision rule), and `side_effects`.
The normal CW-AIP envelope remains the transport; this is its payload guidance,
not a replacement protocol or permission grant.

No pack selected means the existing Council workflow. Integrity or provenance
failure blocks pack loading, rather than falling back to an unpinned download.
