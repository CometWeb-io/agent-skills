# Reporting-guideline families

Use reporting guidelines as **reporting completeness aids**, not as automatic scientific-validity verdicts. A paper can satisfy a checklist and still have a weak inference; a noncompliant report may describe a sound procedure incompletely.

Common families to consider when clearly applicable include:

- randomized trials — CONSORT family;
- observational studies — STROBE family;
- systematic reviews/meta-analyses — PRISMA family;
- prediction/model studies — TRIPOD family and relevant AI extensions;
- diagnostic accuracy — STARD family;
- economic evaluations — CHEERS family;
- animal research — ARRIVE family;
- qualitative research — use a domain-appropriate reporting guideline rather than forcing a quantitative checklist;
- protocols — use the corresponding protocol extension/family when applicable.

## Safe use

1. Do not guess the applicable guideline solely from a title.
2. Prefer the manuscript's declared design and journal requirements.
3. If the current version or extension matters, verify it externally in `VERIFY_EXTERNAL` mode.
4. Treat a missing checklist item as `NOT_REPORTED` unless the source proves the procedure was absent.
5. Do not convert checklist compliance into an acceptance recommendation.
