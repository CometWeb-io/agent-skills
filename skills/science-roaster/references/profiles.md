# Science review profiles

Profiles alter the attack surface; they do not change the shared evidence discipline.

## `EXPERIMENTAL`

Prioritize randomization/allocation, intervention fidelity, controls, blinding where applicable, unit of assignment versus analysis, attrition, protocol deviations, multiplicity, and estimand alignment.

## `OBSERVATIONAL`

Prioritize selection, confounding, measurement error, temporal ordering, collider/conditioning risk, missingness, model specification, sensitivity to adjustment choices, and causal-language discipline.

## `PREDICTIVE`

Prioritize data split, leakage, target definition, calibration, discrimination/error metrics, comparator strength, hyperparameter tuning, internal/external validation, missingness handling, class imbalance, and deployment population shift.

## `VALIDATION`

Prioritize target construct, reference/ground truth quality, calibration, uncertainty propagation, repeatability, system/time boundary, comparator baselines, agreement/error definitions, holdout integrity, and transport across devices/settings/populations.

## `METHODS`

Prioritize whether the proposed method solves a clearly defined problem, reference/comparator quality, assumptions, robustness, ablation, failure modes, computational/statistical validity, and whether novelty is methodological rather than terminological.

## `COMPUTATIONAL`

Prioritize data provenance, preprocessing, leakage, determinism/seeds, environment/package versions, algorithmic assumptions, numerical stability, benchmark comparability, and reproducibility assets.

## `REVIEW`

Prioritize search strategy, eligibility, screening, extraction, bias appraisal, synthesis method, heterogeneity, publication bias, evidence certainty, and whether conclusions exceed included-study quality.

## `PROTOCOL`

Prioritize falsifiability, outcome/estimand definition, analysis population, sample/precision rationale, missingness plan, multiplicity, stopping/exclusion rules, governance, reproducibility plan, and whether the design can answer the stated question.

## `MIXED`

Name the constituent profiles and apply only their relevant attack surfaces. Do not use MIXED as a shortcut around choosing a primary design logic.
