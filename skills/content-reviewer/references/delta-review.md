# Delta review

Use DELTA only when a real baseline candidate exists.

1. Fix baseline and current candidate identities.
2. Identify changed sections and dependent sections.
3. Re-run prior findings touched by those changes.
4. Re-open a previously closed finding if the current candidate reproduces the failure mode.
5. Do not re-review untouched surfaces unless dependency/interaction risk makes them material.

A delta review can reduce scope, not evidence quality. Candidate mismatch makes old evidence historical context, not current proof.
