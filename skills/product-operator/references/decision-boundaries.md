# Decision boundaries

Product Operator sequences work. It does not silently become the decision-maker for unresolved consequential trade-offs.

## Classification order

1. **Binding decision already exists and is still valid** -> execute/verify it; do not reopen it just because another option looks attractive.
2. **A missing fact/gate must be known before choosing** -> `VERIFY NOW` first.
3. **A material unresolved choice remains after facts are adequate** -> `DECISION NOW` + specialist handoff.
4. **Choice is routine, reversible, low-risk implementation detail inside an existing decision** -> Product Operator may sequence it as `NOW/NEXT`.

## DECISION NOW domains

Use for unresolved choices that materially affect:
- pricing, tiers, packaging, monetization;
- free vs paid pilot or offer structure;
- ICP/market entry/positioning motion;
- portfolio allocation or strategic product focus;
- high-lock-in architecture;
- material legal, financial, security, privacy, or reputation trade-offs;
- strategic GO/NO-GO under uncertainty.

If an authoritative gatekeeper must establish what is permitted, that fact-finding is `VERIFY NOW`, not a Product Operator recommendation.

## Required behavior

For each `DECISION NOW` item preserve:
- exact question;
- why it changes the critical path;
- at least two unresolved options;
- relevant evidence/conflict;
- exact specialist/gatekeeper;
- what counts as a resolved/binding decision.

Do not select, recommend, or imply the winning option inside Product Operator. After a specialist/user decision returns, re-enter Product Operator and sequence the consequences.

## Examples

- `Free vs paid pilot?` -> `DECISION NOW`; Pricing/Offers for domain analysis, AI Council when the GTM/revenue trade-off is consequential.
- `D-020 pricing vs live pricing` -> preserve conflict; decide whether to retain or supersede through Pricing/AI Council. Do not choose the live number because code is newer.
- `Can we legally accept this payment now?` -> `VERIFY NOW` with the proper legal/finance authority. Only after that fact is known decide the commercial motion if still unresolved.
- `Which test file should be edited to verify an already-approved flow?` -> routine sequencing, not `DECISION NOW`.
