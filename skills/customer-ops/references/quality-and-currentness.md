# Close the customer-visible loop, not just the ticket

Maintainer review: 2026-09-12. This date does not certify live task inputs.

Reconstruct event time, observation time, customer impact, affected accounts, ownership,
and the next promised action. Do not replace missing evidence with a severity score.
Severity is about demonstrated impact and exposure; priority also includes urgency and
constraints. A loud customer or valuable logo is not proof of a wider incident.

Calculate service deadlines only from the actual contract/policy, timezone, service
calendar, pause rules, and incident clock. Unknown policy means unknown compliance;
do not hard-code a universal response time, refund right, or breach notification window.
For legal implications verify the relevant jurisdiction and current primary authority.

Separate reproduced, mitigated, fixed, deployed, and customer-verified. A merged fix
or internal green test is not customer closure. Verify the original symptom in the
relevant version/environment and record residual risk and a recurrence watch condition.

Before sending or changing CRM/support state, check explicit authority, recipient,
account, source version, duplicate action, and idempotency. Drafts are not sent messages.
Minimize personal data in engineering handoffs; preserve reproducibility without
copying credentials. Do not reveal one customer's data while investigating another.

## Evidence that would block acceptance

Stop approval when a decisive claim lacks an inspectable source, a required operation
was not executed, a protected invariant changes, or validation fails. Return the
bounded result and the specific missing evidence; do not fill the gap with confidence.

## Task-time source checks

Open the applicable current primary/system-of-record source before relying on a volatile
claim. Record actual version, effective/observed time, scope, and retrieval limitations.
The shared source-review ledger schedules maintenance; it cannot establish currentness
of every operational claim. Stable methodological guidance above is an editorial
operating policy, not a claim of a new universal standard.

No external product/legal facts are hard-coded in this domain supplement. Live
operational facts must come from the systems of record selected for the task.
